Pillar I

Assistive Tool, Not Human Replacement

verified_user Explicit Ethical Boundary

SignConnect is designed for everyday conversational moments and situational needs. It is NOT a substitute for certified human Indian Sign Language interpreters in complex medical, legal, judicial, or educational settings.

We actively advocate for certified interpreters in all institutional environments to protect linguistic justice and legal parity.

Indian Sign Language (ISL) Sovereignty

ISL is a rich, natural language with its own complex syntax, facial expressions, and spatial grammar—distinct from ASL or BSL.
